A client needs one prefix, `vbm_`, and two hosts: MCP at `https://mcpbrain.valor.digital/mcp` and REST at `https://valorbrain-api.valor.digital`. The tenant always comes from the credential: do not send `X-Tenant-ID` and do not invent a tenant UUID. Use one token per agent or persona, so writes stay attributable and revoking one does not interrupt the others.

To connect a client step by step, see [Connect](/en/docs/integrations/connect).

## Credentials

The credentials are not interchangeable. Each refusal below is the server's exact answer; the [error catalog](/en/docs/api/errors) says what to do about each one. No client credential opens the admin, credit and subscription routes or `/metrics`.

## Per-credential detail

### MCP token (`vbm_`)

- **Issued by:** Agent-native signup (`mcp_token` in the response) — `npx -y @valorbrain/cli@latest init --agent --agent-caller <id> --json (POST /api/v1/agents/signup)`; App → Settings → MCP Tokens — `https://valorbrain.valor.digital/settings/mcp-tokens (POST /api/v1/mcp/tokens)`; Connect device flow (one token per harness) — `npx -y @valorbrain/connect (POST /api/v1/cli/device/code no app)`; OAuth 2.1 (consent, code + PKCE) — `POST /oauth/token grant_type=authorization_code`; EMA: company IdP assertion — `POST /oauth/token grant_type=urn:ietf:params:oauth:grant-type:jwt-bearer`.
- **Stored in:** `mcp_tokens.token_hash (sha256)`.
- **Tenant:** `mcp_tokens.tenant_id` (a header never switches the tenant).
- **User:** `mcp_tokens.user_id`; persona from `X-Source-Agent` or `clientInfo`.
- **Scope:** Token scopes (`read`, `write`, `admin`, granular ones from src/mcp-scopes.ts), toolset, allowed collections and audience (RFC 8707).
- **Validity:** No expiry when created in the app. Signup: 30 days until the claim, which removes the expiry. OAuth: up to the consenter's validity or `VALORBRAIN_OAUTH_TOKEN_MAX_TTL_DAYS`. EMA: the issuer's `token_ttl_seconds` (default 900 s). ([revocation · rotation](/en/docs/api/token-lifecycle#mcp-token))
- **Rate limit:** `authenticated` and `search`, per sha256 of the token ([rate limit](/en/docs/api/rate-limits))

### Agent account key (`vb_agent_`)

- **Issued by:** Agent-native signup (`api_key` in the response) — `POST /api/v1/agents/signup`.
- **Stored in:** `shadow_tenants.api_key_hash (sha256)`.
- **Tenant:** `shadow_tenants.tenant_id`, only while `tenants.billing_status = 'active'`.
- **User:** `shadow_tenants.default_user_id` (`agent-default`).
- **Scope:** The whole workspace; capped at `read write` on the OAuth consent.
- **Validity:** 30 days from signup unless someone claims the workspace; after the claim it does not expire. ([revocation · rotation](/en/docs/api/token-lifecycle#agent-key))
- **Rate limit:** `authenticated` and `search`, per sha256 of the key ([rate limit](/en/docs/api/rate-limits))

### Service key (`vb_`)

- **Issued by:** Tenant provisioning (`saas-service`) — `POST /api/v1/admin/tenants/:id/api-tokens (rota de administração da plataforma)`; CLI/valorcode device flow in the app — `POST /api/v1/cli/device/token no app`.
- **Stored in:** `api_tokens.token_hash (sha256)`.
- **Tenant:** `api_tokens.tenant_id`, only while `tenants.billing_status = 'active'`.
- **User:** `api_tokens.user_id`.
- **Scope:** Implicit role scopes; capped at `read write` on the OAuth consent.
- **Validity:** `api_tokens.expires_at` when set; no expiry otherwise. ([revocation · rotation](/en/docs/api/token-lifecycle#service-key))
- **Rate limit:** `authenticated` and `search`, per sha256 of the key ([rate limit](/en/docs/api/rate-limits))

### Workspace ingest key (`fk_`)

- **Issued by:** App → Settings → API keys — `https://valorbrain.valor.digital/settings/api-keys`.
- **Stored in:** `SaaS api_keys (hash do segredo)`.
- **Tenant:** The workspace that owns the key in the SaaS.
- **User:** Carries no user; writes are attributed to the workspace.
- **Scope:** Ingest only.
- **Validity:** The key's expiry when set; no expiry otherwise. ([revocation · rotation](/en/docs/api/token-lifecycle#ingest-key))
- **Rate limit:** `saas_ingest`, per credential ([rate limit](/en/docs/api/rate-limits))

### Dashboard session

- **Issued by:** App login — `https://valorbrain.valor.digital/login`.
- **Stored in:** `cookie HttpOnly no navegador`.
- **Tenant:** The session's active workspace.
- **User:** The signed-in user.
- **Scope:** The member's role in the workspace.
- **Validity:** The app session's validity; not an integration contract. ([revocation · rotation](/en/docs/api/token-lifecycle#saas-session))
- **Rate limit:** App limits ([rate limit](/en/docs/api/rate-limits))
