---

The app ships five OAuth connectors: **Notion, Google Drive, Slack, GitHub,
Linear**. They are BYOK — you paste the OAuth client for your workspace.
Sync runs in the background and ingests into the tenant's engine.

They live at [valorbrain.valor.digital/connectors](https://valorbrain.valor.digital/connectors)
after login. They require **Starter or above**, not the free plan.

This is not MCP. Connectors fill collections; agents then retrieve over MCP
or REST.

Filesystem watchers and webhooks are additional ingest paths on
self-hosted engines. Hosted customers who just need "put this document in
the brain" should use CLI `add`, MCP `memory_store`, or
`POST /api/v1/ingest` with an `fk_` key.
