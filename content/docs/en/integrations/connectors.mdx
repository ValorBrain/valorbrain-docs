The app ships eight connectors: **Notion, Google Drive, Slack, GitHub, Linear,
HubSpot, Jira and Granatum**. They are BYOK — you bring your workspace's
credential. Notion, GitHub and Linear take an OAuth client or an access token;
Google Drive and Slack take an OAuth client only; HubSpot, Jira and Granatum
take an access token only. Sync runs in the background and ingests into the
tenant's engine.

They live at [valorbrain.valor.digital/connectors](https://valorbrain.valor.digital/connectors)
after login. They require a **paid plan**; the free plan has no connectors.

This is not MCP. Connectors fill collections; agents then retrieve over MCP
or REST.

Filesystem watchers and webhooks are additional ingest paths on on-premise
(Enterprise) installations. Hosted customers who just need "put this document in
the brain" should use CLI `add`, MCP `memory_store`, or
`POST /api/v1/ingest` with an `fk_` key.

## claude.ai and Claude Desktop (custom connector)

The claude.ai and Claude Desktop "custom connector" is a different thing: it
is MCP. Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector → URL
`https://mcpbrain.valor.digital/mcp`. Sign-in is OAuth; there is no token to
paste. The other clients are in [connect.md](https://valorbrain.com.br/connect.md)
and the [Quickstart](/en/docs/quickstart).
