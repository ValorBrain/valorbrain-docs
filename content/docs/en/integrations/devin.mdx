---
title: Devin
description: Custom MCP over HTTP with OAuth, so each person signs in as themselves.
---

Devin keeps custom MCPs at three scopes of **Customize → MCPs**:
**Personal**, **Organization** and **Enterprise**. An account without an
organization only has Personal. At any scope the right path is OAuth: each
person signs in to ValorBrain with their own account, what Devin writes stays
with whoever is in the session, and there is no token to paste.

## 1. Add the server

Pick the scope:

- **Personal**: anyone adds it for their own sessions;
- **Organization** or **Enterprise**: once for everyone, by someone with the
  **Manage MCP Servers** permission.

Then:

1. Open [Customize → MCPs](https://app.devin.ai/customize?tab=mcps) at that
   scope.
2. **Add MCP** → **Add custom MCP**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Server Name**: `ValorBrain`;
   - **Short Description**: `Company memory`;
   - **Transport**: `HTTP`;
   - **Server URL**: `https://mcpbrain.valor.digital/mcp`;
   - **Authentication method**: `OAuth`;
   - **Access** (Organization or Enterprise only): `Personal`, so each member
     authorizes their own account.
4. **Save**.

Devin registers its own OAuth client (dynamic client registration) and uses
its own callback; there is no OAuth app to create in ValorBrain.

## 2. Connect

In **Customize → MCPs**, open ValorBrain and click **Connect**. The browser
opens the ValorBrain consent screen: sign in with your account and approve.
From then on your Devin sessions use the memory with your identity and
permissions. On an organization server, each member does their own
**Connect**.

Without an account yet, create one at
[valorbrain.valor.digital](https://valorbrain.valor.digital/auth/register)
before **Connect**.

## 3. Verify

In **Test tools**, Devin lists the ValorBrain tools. In a session, ask it to
call `memory_prepare`: it returns who you are in the workspace and the memory
relevant to the task.

## Who else acts through your connection

Devin itself warns that **Personal** access is not a security boundary:
other people in the organization can interact with your sessions. Whoever
does acts, through Devin, with your ValorBrain identity and sees your private
memory. Agree with your team on who joins whose sessions.

## What about a token in the header?

Devin also accepts an **Auth Header** (`Authorization: Bearer <vbm_…>`). The
header is fixed and applies to every session of the scope where the server
was added:

- at **Personal** scope, with your own personal token, it works: only you use
  it;
- at **Organization** or **Enterprise** scope, with one person's token, every
  member would act as the token's owner, with their permissions and private
  documents;
- a gateway token does not fit either: without a per-person `X-User-ID`, it
  reads the whole workspace.
