---
title: Devin
description: Organization custom MCP over HTTP, with Personal-access OAuth.
---

Devin adds remote MCP servers per organization. That is why the right path is
OAuth with **Personal** access: each member signs in to ValorBrain as
themselves, and what Devin writes stays with the person of the session. There
is no token to paste.

## 1. Add the server (organization admin)

Needs the **Manage MCP Servers** permission in Devin.

1. Open [Customize → MCPs](https://app.devin.ai/customize?tab=mcps).
2. **Add MCP** → **Add custom MCP**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Server Name**: `ValorBrain`;
   - **Transport**: `HTTP`;
   - **Server URL**: `https://mcpbrain.valor.digital/mcp`;
   - **Authentication method**: `OAuth`, with **Access** `Personal`.
4. **Save**.

Devin registers its own client (dynamic client registration) and uses its own
callback; there is no OAuth app to create in ValorBrain.

## 2. Connect (each member)

In **Customize → MCPs**, open ValorBrain and click **Connect**. The browser
opens the ValorBrain consent screen: sign in with your account and approve.
From then on that person's Devin sessions use the memory with their identity
and permissions.

Without an account yet, create one at
[valorbrain.valor.digital](https://valorbrain.valor.digital/auth/register)
before **Connect**.

## 3. Verify

In **Test tools**, Devin lists the ValorBrain tools. In a session, ask it to
call `memory_prepare`: it returns who you are in the workspace and the memory
relevant to the task.

## Why not a token in the header

Devin also accepts an **Auth Header** (`Authorization: Bearer <vbm_…>`), but
that header applies to the whole organization: every member would act as the
token's owner, with their permissions and private documents. Use the header
only in a one-person organization. A gateway token does not fit either:
without a per-person `X-User-ID`, it reads the whole workspace.
